## English

UmaSchedule ("the App") does **not collect, store, or transmit** any personal data.

### 1. Information We Collect
None. The App has no accounts or login and collects no personal information (name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers, etc.).

### 2. Data Stored On Device
App preferences (category order, language, notification settings) are stored **only on your device** and are never transmitted. They are removed when you delete the App.

### 3. Network Use
The App downloads schedule information **read-only** from a public data file (JSON hosted on GitHub). No user-identifying information is sent during this process.

### 4. Third Parties / Analytics / Ads
The App uses **no** analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking, and shares no data with third parties.

### 5. Children's Privacy
The App collects no personal data, including from children.

### 6. Disclaimer
This is an unofficial schedule app and is not affiliated with Kakao Games / Cygames.
